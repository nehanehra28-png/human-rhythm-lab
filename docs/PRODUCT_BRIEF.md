# Product brief

## Purpose and audience

Human Rhythm Lab guides invited testers through a 30-day wellness experience with daily content, personal check-ins, progress tracking, and weekly AI reflections. It is not a diagnostic or treatment service.

Participants have private dashboards. Admins publish content, prepare the shared diet plan, view all participant progress, and add meeting links.

## Confirmed journey

1. Receive an invitation, sign in, and join the cohort.
2. Follow guided experiences on days 1–14.
3. Attend a midpoint feedback call using a Zoom or Teams link.
4. Follow the shared diet plan on days 15–28.
5. Complete closing review and feedback on days 29–30.
6. Review recorded patterns and AI reflections weekly.

Each experience includes team-created written content, video, and an infographic. Tone is warm and supportive. Supplied health-related sample content remains draft material requiring qualified review before publication.

## Proposed success criteria

Testers can find today's activity, record a quick check-in, review their week, and access the call without assistance. Admins can publish content and understand cohort progress. Missing and skipped activities are distinguished.

## MVP boundaries

No wearables, social feed, payments, public registration, automated diet generation, medical diagnosis, AI coaching chat, in-app booking, or in-app media generation.
